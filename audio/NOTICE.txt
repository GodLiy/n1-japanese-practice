Japanese recordings in this folder were generated offline with Piper 1.8
and the ja_JP hi-fi-captain medium voice (female speaker 0, male speaker 1).
The voice was trained on the NICT Hi-Fi-CAPTAIN corpus:
https://ast-astrec.nict.go.jp/en/release/hi-fi-captain/
That corpus is published under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
These files are for non-commercial language practice.
Playback does not send practice text or the user's voice to a TTS service.
Piper: https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl
